About HotelWala

HotelWala is a lightweight hotel and restaurant management app designed to make table and service handling simple and efficient. No bookings, no payment gateways, no location tracking—just a clean and easy-to-use solution for hotels and restaurants.

Developed by WorldTechWala Developers, this app helps staff manage tables, track orders, and generate bills without complicated features or unnecessary permissions.

✔ Easy Table Management

Organize tables and assign customers with a few clicks.

✔ Fast Billing

Generate accurate bills quickly with minimal steps.

✔ Order Tracking

Track pending and completed orders in real-time.

✔ No Extra Permissions

No camera, storage, location, or payment integrations needed.

✔ Simple & Secure

Privacy-focused app with zero data sharing and minimal resources.

✔ Works Offline

No internet dependency—manage everything offline.
