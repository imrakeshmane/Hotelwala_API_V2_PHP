HotelWala - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2025

Welcome to HotelWala, developed by WorldTechWala Developers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your privacy when you use our application. We value your trust and are committed to ensuring your data remains safe and confidential.

1. Information We Collect

HotelWala is designed as a simple hotel table and service management app. We do not collect unnecessary personal data. However, the following information may be collected:

  • Basic Details: If you choose to create an account, we may collect your name, email, or contact number for identification and communication purposes.
  • Technical Information: App usage data (like crash logs or performance metrics) to improve stability and functionality.

We do not collect sensitive information such as financial data, personal identification numbers, location data, or biometric data.

2. How We Use the Information

We use the collected data only to make your experience better. This includes:

  • Managing hotel tables and related services efficiently within the app.
  • Improving app performance, troubleshooting issues, and fixing bugs.
  • Sending important notifications related to updates or essential alerts (only if you enable notifications).

3. Data Sharing

Your privacy is our priority. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third-party organizations for advertising or marketing purposes. Data is only used internally to provide and improve the service.

4. Permissions

HotelWala is designed to be simple and secure. The app:

  • Does not access your camera, storage, or photo gallery.
  • Does not use location services, maps, geocoding, or GPS.
  • Does not integrate payment gateways or financial services.
  • Uses only minimal permissions required for basic operations such as notifications and Bluetooth connect.

5. Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no digital platform can guarantee 100% security, so we recommend using secure devices and networks.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals above the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that a child has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or app features. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the “Last Updated” date will be revised accordingly.
